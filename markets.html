Market cap is circulating supply × price, published by Hyperliquid for spot tokens; a perp shows one when its own spot token is listed. Blank means not known, never zero. Revenue is protocol revenue from DefiLlama, credited to the token of the protocol that earned it; fees are what its users paid. Stocks show revenue reported to the SEC, by quarter (a fiscal fourth quarter, marked *, is the annual report less the three quarters before it); companies that do not file with the SEC show none. Categories: Hyperliquid's own, DefiLlama's for revenue tokens, and our own sectors. Strict shows the markets someone actually trades: every perp, live HIP-3 markets, and spot tokens that trade, earn, or are on our lists.
