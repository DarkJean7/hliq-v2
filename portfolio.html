How this is worked out: one price per day, Hyperliquid's daily close; a day with no trade keeps the last close. Leverage scales the whole basket, and a balance that reaches zero is liquidated and stays there. Daily closes miss intraday wicks, so leveraged results are better than a real account would have had. A fee is charged on every trade; funding and slippage are not included. A basket can only be tested from the day its newest holding was listed. The trading strategies are the app's Trade Simulator: each holding runs the rule on its own share of the capital, in its own direction, and a trade still open at the end is not counted. Past prices say nothing certain about future ones; this is a way to look at history, not advice.
