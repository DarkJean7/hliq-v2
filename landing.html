> bots that don't sleep

Bots run on our server, not in your browser tab. Close the app, lose signal, put the phone down, and they keep working. They pick up where they left off after a restart, and a grid budgets its margin up front, so its ladder can't grow past what you allowed.

> keys that trade, never withdraw

Trading is signed by a Hyperliquid agent key you approve once. Hyperliquid itself won't let an agent key move money: withdrawals and sends always need your own wallet, and you can revoke the key on the exchange whenever you want.
