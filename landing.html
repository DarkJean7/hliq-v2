01 · Features

Everything the exchange can do,
laid out for the way you trade.

◎

All Accounts

Every wallet you own merged into one equity, one PnL and one position list, and you can act on all of them at once. Each order is signed by the right wallet's key.

♥

Health that matches HL

Liquidation risk from Hyperliquid's own unified-account ratio, per collateral token, across every dex. You see the book that runs out first, not an average.

⚡

Pro order types

Chase, Scale, Stop & Take, Trailing and TWAP: the orders a desk uses, in a sheet you can work with your thumb.

◈

HIP-3 & outcomes

Builder-deployed perps like stocks and gold sit next to the main book, and prediction outcomes get their own tab.

⏰

Alerts that wake you

Push price alerts, plus a wake alarm that rings even with the screen locked when a level you care about breaks.

▦

Journal & calendar

A PnL calendar, a trade journal with pictures, and a "what moved your account" breakdown by asset.

03 · Security

Keys that can trade.
Never keys that can withdraw.

Agent keys only

Trading is signed by a Hyperliquid agent key you approve. Hyperliquid itself does not let an agent key withdraw or transfer funds.

Your wallet moves money

Deposits, withdrawals and sends always need your own wallet's signature. We never hold the key that can move your money.

Revoke any time

An agent key is one approval on Hyperliquid, and you can revoke it from the exchange whenever you want.
